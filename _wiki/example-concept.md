# Activation Friction

*Synthesized from 12 sources across work and personal wings.*

## Pattern

Activation friction describes the gap between a user's first contact with a system and their first moment of perceived value. The longer and more confusing the path to that moment, the higher the churn in that window.

## Cross-domain appearances

This concept appears in product work (onboarding flows), leadership (new hire ramp time), and personal notes (learning new skills). The structural similarity: value is gated behind an unfamiliar process.

## Key insight

The most effective interventions collapse time-to-value rather than smoothing friction itself — surface the payoff earlier, not smoother onboarding steps.

## Related concepts

- `time-to-value` — the measure; activation-friction is the cause
- `expectation-misalignment` — often co-occurs; users expect faster value
- `onboarding-design` — the design discipline that addresses it
